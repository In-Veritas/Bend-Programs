Aquarium

How it works
• Click — or hold — above the waterline to pour eggs. They fall in, then sink.
• An egg left alone hatches into a fry (brown) — fry school together and eat eggs.
• A fry that ate 12 eggs becomes a juvenile (green); it eats 5 fry to become an adult (blue). Eggs don't grow it.
• A fish eats once per second, and only sets off to hunt another fish once every ten seconds.
• Adults eat ANY fish up to half their size — even other adults — and keep growing until they shatter the tank.
• Your score is the number of ADULT fish alive when it breaks. R restarts.
The simulation and every pixel of the tank are computed by the Bend program (main.bend: Aq.step / Aq.view); this page only forwards events and paints the image.
