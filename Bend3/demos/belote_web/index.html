Classic Belote at this table

  1. Four seats form fixed teams: North–South against East–West. You play South; Bend controls the other three seats.
  2. Five cards are dealt before bidding. The first round offers only the turned card’s suit. If everyone passes, the second round offers any other suit.
  3. After a take, everyone receives eight cards. Follow suit. When void, trump unless your partner currently wins; when trumping, beat the highest trump if your hand can.
  4. Trump order is J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Other suits use A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7. The final trick adds ten points.
  5. The taking team must score more card points than the defense. A held trump king–queen is automatically announced as Belote/Rebelote for 20 points. This edition omits tierce/carré declarations.
